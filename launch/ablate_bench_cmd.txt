#   0.3 us is flagged (ABLATE_NOTE), not a gate. Control D0 vs CLAIMS 205 CTRL (+0.85 / +2.96) differing by > 0.4 us is
#   flagged, not a gate.

#   stated per line): cut(V) >= 0.3 us. Otherwise NO.
